# Game concept

You're a process. Your mission is to eliminate another players' processes. Kinda RPG like game, you have skills, stats and methods to upgrade them.

The Enviroment in which you play is the computer. You have computation units as your main resource you can gain them by doing tasks and killing opponents or by farming.

Tasks is the computer initialized tasks that are stored in another "dead processes". It may inclute solving some alg problems or etc. After solving you can capture this process as yours where you can run some piece of software that or make/upgrade your skills or improve your stats.

Main skills:
Locator-family - to scan processes.
Terminator-family - to attack.
Protector - to protect processes.
